About

Teaching with clarity, designing with empathy

Mousumi is a computer engineer from North South University (BSc CSE, 2020) and a working teacher in Dhaka. She currently teaches O Level Computer Science at Wisdom Academy, alongside earlier classroom work in mathematics and science. She brings patient communication, empathic listening, and a practical sense of structure to the classroom — and to how she thinks about interfaces and small digital products.

Away from school, she gardens, reads Humayun Ahmed and Dan Brown, and runs Avocado Lifestyle BD, a Facebook page promoting traditional products of Bangladesh.

She works comfortably in English and colloquial Bengali, with a growing command of Spanish.
